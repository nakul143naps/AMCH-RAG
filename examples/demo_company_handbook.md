# Northstar Analytics — Fictional Employee Handbook

This synthetic handbook exists only for the AMCH-RAG portfolio demo. Northstar Analytics is fictional.

## Remote work

Employees may work remotely for up to two days per week with prior manager approval. Teams should coordinate schedules so customer support coverage remains available. Company equipment must be used for company work.

## Expenses

Employees must submit business expense reports within 10 calendar days after the expense. Each report should include an itemized receipt, the business purpose, and the relevant project code. A manager reviews submissions before reimbursement.

## Annual leave

Full-time employees receive 20 working days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Leave requests should be submitted to the manager at least five working days in advance when practical. Unused leave does not automatically carry into the next calendar year.

## Onboarding

New employees are paired with an onboarding buddy for their first month. The buddy helps them find internal tools, meet teammates, and get answers to routine first-month questions. The hiring manager remains responsible for role expectations and performance feedback.
